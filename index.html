CandySort

Puzzle game · Android · Free

The sweetest color-sorting puzzle. Pour, sort and match delicious candies until every jar holds a single color. Simple to learn, wonderfully relaxing, and surprisingly addictive — the perfect way to unwind while giving your brain a gentle workout.

No timers Works offline Free to play

How to play

  • Tap a jar to pour its top candies into another
  • Only matching colors can stack together
  • Sort every jar into a single color to clear the level
  • Think ahead — a little planning goes a long way

Features

  • 100 handcrafted levels, from easy to brain-teasing
  • Smooth, satisfying candy animations
  • Relaxing sound effects and soft background music
  • A cute shop: unlock candy skins, jars and backgrounds
  • Daily rewards and gift chests to collect
  • Helpful hints and an extra-jar booster when you get stuck
  • Play 100% offline — no Wi-Fi required

No timers, no pressure — just calm, colorful puzzles you can enjoy anytime. Whether you have five minutes or a whole evening, CandySort is the cozy brain break you deserve.

CareReminder

Health & wellbeing · Android · Free

Recurring reminders that actually stick. Organise them into lists — drink water, move, take your medication — set how often each one repeats, and keep track of what you actually did.

Fully offline No account Free to use

Features

  • Flexible frequency: minutes, hours, days or weeks
  • Quiet hours, so you're never woken up at night
  • Pick the days of the week for each reminder
  • “Done / Missed” tracking with progress charts
  • Organise reminders into colorful lists
  • Available in English, French, Spanish and German
  • 100% private — everything stays on your device
