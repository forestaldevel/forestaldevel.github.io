CareReminder

Recurring reminders that actually stick. Organise them into lists — drink water, move, take your medication — and track what you did.

  • Flexible frequency: minutes, hours, days, weeks
  • Quiet hours so you're never woken up at night
  • Pick the days of the week for each reminder
  • “Done / Missed” tracking with progress charts
  • 100% private — everything stays on your device
Get it on Google Play

Privacy

CareReminder stores all of your data locally on your device and does not send it to any server.
